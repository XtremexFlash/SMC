ABOUT US

Sigma Machinery Corporation

Established in 1977, Sigma Machinery Corporation has built a legacy of quality, excellence, and trust in the industrial machinery and engineering industry. With decades of experience and a commitment to customer satisfaction, we have earned a reputation for reliability, integrity, and lasting business relationships.

Our journey began under the vision and guidance of the late Shri Lalchand Golani, who laid the foundation of the company in 1977. His legacy was carried forward by Shri Jai Kishan Golani, who continued to strengthen the company's foundations. Today, the company is in the capable hands of Mr. Dinesh Golani, continuing this tradition of excellence while adapting to the evolving needs of modern industry.

We specialize in a comprehensive range of industrial machinery and precision workholding solutions, including Chucks, Vices, Bandsaw Machines, and Lathes. Our products are selected with a strong emphasis on precision, durability, dependable performance, and compliance with industry standards.

At Sigma Machinery Corporation, our responsibility extends beyond supplying quality products. We believe in providing dependable after-sales service and maintaining lasting relationships with our customers through consistent support, transparency, and trust.

For us, success is measured not merely by transactions, but by the relationships we build and the confidence our customers place in us. We strive to establish partnerships that endure for decades, founded on mutual trust, quality, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

With a legacy spanning nearly five decades, we remain dedicated to the principles on which our company was founded.

Sigma Machinery Corporation — Built on Legacy. Driven by Quality. Trusted for Generations.
